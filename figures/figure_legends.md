**Figure 1 | Study design and the six groups.** **a**, Participant flow (STROBE). **b**, Each group: n (unweighted), survey-weighted prevalence among US adults aged ≥40 years (95% CI) and deaths through 2019 among participants eligible for linkage. Body charts are shaded by the survey-weighted share of the group reporting pain or tingling at each site (hands and feet, front view; low back, back view), with the shares below; feet only, foot symptoms without hand symptoms or low back pain. Glycemic status is among adults with known status. SMN, symptomatic, monofilament-normal.

**Figure 2 | A model trained only on insensate adults without symptoms scores the SMN group above the negative controls.** **a**, Neuropathy-similarity scores (logit) by group: density, individual adults, interquartile range and median; dashed line, reference median. The two training groups have five-fold out-of-fold scores; the other groups are scored by the average of the five fold models. **b**, Survey-weighted differences in mean score versus reference, adjusted for age, sex and race and ethnicity. Main model: point estimate, bootstrap distribution and 66% (thick) and 95% (thin) bias-corrected percentile intervals from 1,000 Rao–Wu bootstrap replicates of the whole pipeline (model refitting, scoring and regression; primary sampling units resampled within strata). Smaller symbols, design-based 95% CIs (Taylor linearization, scores fixed) for a model without glycemic features, adults without diabetes and the held-out 2003–04 cycle (model trained on 1999–2002). The primary contrast, SMN minus back pain only, is also expressed as a percentage of the score gap between insensate adults without symptoms and the reference group. SMN, symptomatic, monofilament-normal.

**Figure 3 | Biomarker profiles of the six groups.** Survey-weighted standardized mean difference (SMD) of each of 40 routine clinical and laboratory measures versus the reference group, after survey-weighted adjustment for age, sex and race and ethnicity; biomarkers are ordered by the SMN estimate. **a**, SMN group with 95% CI; colored bars and grey shading mark biomarkers whose 95% CI excludes 0. **b**, All groups (n, group size): circle color, SMD; circle area, |SMD|; outlined circles, 95% CI excludes 0; faded circles, 95% CI includes 0. The strip above the matrix gives the biomarker domain. **c**, Number of biomarkers whose 95% CI excludes 0. CIs are bias-corrected percentile intervals from the pipeline bootstrap (Fig. 2). SMN, symptomatic, monofilament-normal.

**Figure 4 | Mortality through 2019.** **a**, Survey-weighted Kaplan–Meier survival by group (shaded, design-based 95% CI) with unweighted numbers at risk and deaths/n. **b**, Survey-weighted Cox hazard ratios versus reference adjusted for age, sex, race and ethnicity, education, income-to-poverty ratio, smoking status, BMI, diabetes and eGFR; design-based 95% CIs (Taylor linearization, design degrees of freedom); thick bars, 66% CI; deaths/n in the model sample. E-value for the SMN group 1.67 (CI limit 1.28). **c**, Cause-specific hazard ratios with 95% CIs (* P < 0.05, ** P < 0.01, *** P < 0.001); tiles colored by log hazard ratio; outlined and bold, 95% CI excludes 1. SMN, symptomatic, monofilament-normal.

**Supplementary Figure S1 | Sensitivity analyses as specification curves.** Each column is one analysis, ordered by its estimate; the dot matrix below shows which analytic choices it makes (shaded column, main analysis). **a**, SMN minus back pain only in the transfer-model score; models are refitted within each restricted sample. **b**, Adjusted hazard ratio for SMN versus reference with back pain only alongside (grey); red choices mark analyses that exclude, adjust for or restrict on peripheral circulation (also marked by triangles). All intervals are design-based (Taylor linearization with design degrees of freedom; scores treated as fixed in a). Thick and thin bars, 66% and 95% CIs; hollow symbols, 95% CI includes the null; dashed line, main estimate; P values are printed between the curve and the choice matrix. High urate, serum urate >6.8 mg/dL or a urate-lowering or gout medication; neurotoxic drugs, a prescription in the past 30 days for an agent listed in Supplementary Table S2; hepatitis C, positive confirmed antibody; thyroid disease, self-reported thyroid disease, thyroid medication or a thyroid-stimulating hormone outside 0.4–4.5 mIU/L (measured in 1999–2002 only).

**Supplementary Figure S2 | Similarity of biomarker profiles.** Each profile is the vector of survey-weighted, age-, sex- and race-adjusted SMDs of the 40 biomarkers in Fig. 3. **a**, Cosine similarity between group profiles with bootstrap 95% CIs (outlined column, similarity with the SMN profile). The similarity of SMN with insensate adults without symptoms minus its similarity with back pain only was 0.16 (−0.05 to 0.36). **b**, Cosine similarity of the insensate-without-symptoms profile with the SMN and control profiles (top) and with the profiles of eight unrelated self-reported conditions (bottom; grey band, their range). Each condition profile compares normal-exam adults without foot symptoms who reported the condition with reference adults who did not; n, adults with the condition. Smaller groups give noisier profiles, which lowers their cosine similarity. 95% CIs are bias-corrected percentile intervals from the pipeline bootstrap.
